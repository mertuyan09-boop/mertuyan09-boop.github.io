Siradan Gorunen Yukleyici

A private, single-user command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Sıradan Görünen to upload and schedule the channel's own Shorts videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0) and grants upload permission.
  2. The tool reads a finished vertical video and its title, description and tags from local files.
  3. It uploads the video to the owner's channel with videos.insert and schedules it with status.publishAt (one Short per day).
  4. The uploaded video ID is saved locally so the same video is never uploaded twice.
